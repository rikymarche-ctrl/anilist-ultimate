# Seasonal Wrapped

Open **Astra → Wrapped → Seasonal Wrapped**, then select a year and broadcast season. The recap reads your authenticated AniList anime list and current Astra scores; you do not need to import every title into Astra first.

The dashboard announces a newly concluded season three days after its end. The story opens when you choose it and stays available from Wrapped. A season still in progress is provisional.

## Season attribution

| Case                                            | Treatment                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Starts and ends in the selected season          | Eligible for the season's concluded-title group.             |
| Starts earlier and ends in the selected season  | A leftover, labeled with its origin season.                  |
| Continues beyond the selected season            | Shown separately, without that season's final score/ranking. |
| Finished in an earlier season but watched later | A recovery, excluded from the later broadcast-season recap.  |

A final ranking requires known finished broadcasting, personal participation and a completed personal status. The score uses Astra when available, otherwise AniList; an unscored title does not receive an invented vote.

Participation uses the supported personal dates, dated local activity or an explicit confirmation. Missing/uncertain dates have their own confirmation flow. Personal dates that clearly place viewing after the broadcast season identify recoveries, subject to available earlier activity.

Winter is January–March, Spring April–June, Summer July–September and Fall October–December. Official end dates decide the concluding season. Unknown, future or incomplete end dates are not inferred from the episode count. Separate AniList media IDs remain separate works.

## Story and personal choices

Navigate the story with arrows, progress segments or the supported keys. The concluded-title list and genre radar use glass panels to keep text and chart labels readable against the background.

Choose personal awards on the interactive slide. The biggest-disappointment choice can include a dropped title. Awards and participation confirmations are saved per account/season and included in current Astra backups.

A failed choice save preserves the selection and offers recovery actions. Export stays disabled while a save or unresolved save error is pending. Opening the recap does not modify AniList ratings, Notes or list entries.

## Sharing and privacy

Private/hidden titles start excluded. Review the displayed titles and awards before enabling their inclusion or sharing a PNG. **Download PNG** saves a card locally; it does not post it. **Open AniChart** opens the selected season on the external catalog.

Continuing titles receive their concluding-season treatment when the relevant data becomes available; reopening a recap does not invent missing history.
